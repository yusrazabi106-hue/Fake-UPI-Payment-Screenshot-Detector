ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is UPI Shield?

🛡️

Don't Trust the Screenshot. Verify the Payment.

UPI Shield is a prototype that inspects payment screenshots for visible inconsistencies and potential manipulation indicators.

01

Upload

Submit a payment screenshot.

02

Inspect

Extract text and run forensic heuristics.

03

Review

See warnings, evidence and limitations.

Important: screenshot analysis cannot prove that money was received. Even a clean-looking screenshot may be fake. Always confirm funds in the merchant's bank account or trusted payment provider. This prototype never labels a screenshot as proof of payment.
